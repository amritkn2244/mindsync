Reading this chart

Mood is scored 1–5: 1 is low, 5 is great. Energy parallels it but often lags a day — low energy today sometimes explains a lower mood tomorrow.

Trends are clues, not verdicts. Two weeks of data is enough to start seeing a useful shape; four weeks is where patterns get trustworthy.
